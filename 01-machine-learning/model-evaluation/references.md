# Model Evaluation: References & Authoritative Sources

Curated textbooks, academic papers, and statistical references covering model validation, metrics, and calibration.

---

## 1. Seminal Publications
- Fawcett, T. (2006). *An introduction to ROC analysis*. **Pattern Recognition Letters**, 27(8), 861–874.
- Davis, J., & Goadrich, M. (2006). *The relationship between Precision-Recall and ROC curves*. In **Proceedings of the 23rd International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML)**, pp. 233–240.
- Guo, C., Pleiss, G., Sun, Y., & Weinberger, K. Q. (2017). *On calibration of modern neural networks*. In **International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML)**, pp. 1321–1330.
- Platt, J. (1999). *Probabilistic outputs for support vector machines and comparisons to regularized likelihood methods*. **Advances in Large Margin Classifiers**, 10(3), 61–74.
- Brier, G. W. (1950). *Verification of forecasts expressed in terms of probability*. **Monthly Weather Review**, 78(1), 1–3.

---

## 2. Textbooks & Engineering Monographs
- **Applied Predictive Modeling** by Max Kuhn and Kjell Johnson (Springer, 2013). Masterclass on model evaluation protocols and performance tuning.
- **Evaluating Learning Algorithms: A Classification Perspective** by Nathalie Japkowicz and Mohak Shah (Cambridge University Press, 2011).
