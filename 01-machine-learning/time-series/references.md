# Time Series Analysis: References & Authoritative Sources

Curated textbooks, seminal papers, and engineering guides covering time-series forecasting, statistical dynamics, and ML sequence models.

---

## 1. Seminal Publications & Foundational Papers
- Box, G. E., & Jenkins, G. M. (1970). *Time Series Analysis: Forecasting and Control*. Holden-Day. (The seminal ARIMA reference).
- Hyndman, R. J., & Koehler, A. B. (2006). *Another look at measures of forecast accuracy*. **International Journal of Forecasting**, 22(4), 679–688. (Foundational MASE paper).
- Dickey, D. A., & Fuller, W. A. (1979). *Distribution of the estimators for autoregressive time series with a unit root*. **Journal of the American Statistical Association**, 74(366a), 427–431.
- Makridakis, S., Spiliotis, E., & Assimakopoulos, V. (2018). *The M4 Competition: Results, findings, conclusion and way forward*. **International Journal of Forecasting**, 34(4), 802–808.

---

## 2. Textbooks & Monographs
- **Forecasting: Principles and Practice** (3rd Edition) by Rob J. Hyndman and George Athanasopoulos (OTexts, 2021). The definitive modern open-access textbook on time series forecasting.
- **Time Series Analysis and Its Applications: With R Examples** (4th Edition) by Robert H. Shumway and David S. Stoffer (Springer, 2017).
