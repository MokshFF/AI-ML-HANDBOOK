# Data Structures for Machine Learning: References & Authoritative Sources

Curated textbooks, academic papers, and computer systems references covering data structures, algorithmic complexity, and spatial search.

---

## 1. Seminal Publications
- Bentley, J. L. (1975). *Multidimensional binary search trees used for associative searching*. **Communications of the ACM**, 18(9), 509–517. [DOI: 10.1145/361002.361007](https://doi.org/10.1145/361002.361007)
- Weinberger, K., Dasgupta, A., Langford, J., Smola, A., & Attenberg, J. (2009). *Feature hashing for large scale multitask learning*. In **Proceedings of the 26th Annual International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML)**, pp. 1113–1120.
- Malkov, Y. A., & Yashunin, D. A. (2018). *Efficient and robust approximate nearest neighbor search using Hierarchical Navigable Small World graphs*. **IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence**, 42(4), 824–836.

---

## 2. Textbooks & Monographs
- **Introduction to Algorithms** (4th Edition) by Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest, and Clifford Stein (MIT Press, 2022). The definitive algorithms reference (CLRS).
- **Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective** (3rd Edition) by Randal E. Bryant and David R. O'Hallaron (Pearson, 2015). Outstanding chapters on CPU cache architectures, memory hierarchies, and spatial locality.
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications** by Martin Kleppmann (O'Reilly Media, 2017). Authoritative treatment of storage engines, B-trees, hash indices, and streaming buffers.
- **Foundations of Machine Learning** (2nd Edition) by Mehryar Mohri, Afshin Rostamizadeh, and Ameet Talwalkar (MIT Press, 2018).
