# Linear Algebra for Machine Learning: References & Authoritative Sources

Curated textbooks, academic papers, and lecture series for linear algebra, matrix computations, and dimensionality reduction.

---

## 1. Seminal Publications & Foundational Papers
- Eckart, C., & Young, G. (1936). *The approximation of one matrix by another of lower rank*. **Psychometrika**, 1(3), 211–218. [DOI: 10.1007/BF02288367](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02288367)
- Pearson, K. (1901). *On lines and planes of closest fit to systems of points in space*. **Philosophical Magazine**, 2(11), 559–572. (Foundational PCA paper).
- Golub, G. H., & Kahan, W. (1965). *Calculating the singular values and pseudo-inverse of a matrix*. **SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis**, 2(2), 205–224.

---

## 2. Textbooks & Monographs
- **Linear Algebra and Learning from Data** by Gilbert Strang (Wellesley-Cambridge Press, 2019). The quintessential textbook connecting linear algebra directly to neural networks and data science.
- **Matrix Computations** (4th Edition) by Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan (Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013). The authoritative bible on numerical linear algebra algorithms.
- **Introduction to Applied Linear Algebra: Vectors, Matrices, and Least Squares** by Stephen Boyd and Lieven Vandenberghe (Cambridge University Press, 2018). Available open-access online.
- **Deep Learning: Chapter 2 (Linear Algebra)** by Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville (MIT Press, 2016).
