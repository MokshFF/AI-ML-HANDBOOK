# Probability & Statistics for Machine Learning: References & Authoritative Sources

Curated textbooks, monographs, and foundational references on probability theory, statistical inference, and experimental design.

---

## 1. Seminal Publications & Foundational Papers
- Student [William Sealy Gosset] (1908). *The probable error of a mean*. **Biometrika**, 6(1), 1–25. (Introduction of the Student's t-distribution).
- Efron, B. (1979). *Bootstrap Methods: Another Look at the Jackknife*. **The Annals of Statistics**, 7(1), 1–26. (The foundational bootstrap paper).
- Benjamini, Y., & Hochberg, Y. (1995). *Controlling the False Discovery Rate: A Practical and Powerful Approach to Multiple Testing*. **Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B**, 57(1), 289–300.

---

## 2. Textbooks & Monographs
- **All of Statistics: A Concise Course in Statistical Inference** by Larry Wasserman (Springer, 2004). The standard reference bridging mathematical statistics and computer science.
- **Statistical Inference** (2nd Edition) by George Casella and Roger L. Berger (Duxbury Press, 2002). Comprehensive classical reference on point estimation, hypothesis testing, and asymptotic theory.
- **Introduction to Probability** (2nd Edition) by Joseph K. Blitzstein and Jessica Hwang (CRC Press, 2019). Intuitive, story-based introduction to conditioned probability, Markov chains, and distributions.
- **Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning: Chapter 1 & 2** by Christopher M. Bishop (Springer, 2006). Canonical explanation of Bayesian probability in ML.
