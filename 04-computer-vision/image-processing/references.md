# Image Processing & Augmentation - References & Further Reading

Foundational citations and seminal papers on spatial image filtering, classical edge detection, and modern deep learning data augmentations.

---

## 1. Seminal Research Papers

- **Mixup: Beyond Empirical Risk Minimization** (2017 / 2018)
  - *Authors*: Hongyi Zhang, Moustapha Cisse, Yann N. Dauphin, David Lopez-Paz
  - *Paper*: [arXiv:1710.09412](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.09412)
  - *Contribution*: Introduced linear interpolation of image pairs and training targets based on Vicinal Risk Minimization.

- **Improved Regularization of Convolutional Neural Networks with Cutout** (2017)
  - *Authors*: Terrance DeVries, Graham W. Taylor
  - *Paper*: [arXiv:1708.04552](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.04552)
  - *Contribution*: Demonstrated that randomly zeroing contiguous square visual patches significantly regularizes deep CNNs.

- **CutMix: Regularization Strategy to Train Strong Classifiers with Localizable Features** (2019)
  - *Authors*: Sangdoo Yun, Dongyoon Han, Seong Joon Oh, Sanghyuk Chun, Junsuk Choe, Youngjoon Yoo
  - *Paper*: [arXiv:1905.04899](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.04899)
  - *Contribution*: Combined spatial cut-and-paste with proportional label mixing, achieving state-of-the-art across ImageNet classification and Pascal VOC localization.

- **AutoAugment: Learning Augmentation Strategies from Data** (2018 / 2019)
  - *Authors*: Ekin D. Cubuk, Barret Zoph, Dandelion Mane, Vijay Vasudevan, Quoc V. Le
  - *Paper*: [arXiv:1805.09501](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.09501)
  - *Contribution*: Formulated data augmentation policy search via reinforcement learning.
