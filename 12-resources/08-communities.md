# AI/ML Professional & Research Communities

High-signal engineering and research communities for peer collaboration and technical discussions.

1. **MLOps Community**
   - *Focus*: Real-world production engineering, feature stores, serving infrastructure, and podcast discussions.
   - *Platform*: Slack community (30k+ practitioners) & global meetups.

2. **Hugging Face Community & Discord**
   - *Focus*: Open-source modeling, dataset curation, demo Spaces, and library support.
   - *Platform*: Discord & Hugging Face Hub discussions.

3. **EleutherAI**
   - *Focus*: Non-profit AI research collective pioneering open large-scale foundation model training (Pythia, GPT-NeoX).
   - *Platform*: Discord & GitHub.

4. **PyTorch Developer Forums**
   - *Focus*: Deep-dive kernel discussions, CUDA development, distributed training bugs, and core RFCs.
   - *Platform*: [discuss.pytorch.org](https://discuss.pytorch.org/).

5. **Reddit r/MachineLearning**
   - *Focus*: High-level discussion of recent research papers, conferences, and industry developments.
   - *Platform*: Reddit (`r/MachineLearning`).

6. **Academic Conferences & Proceedings**
   - **NeurIPS**: Neural Information Processing Systems
   - **ICML**: International Conference on Machine Learning
   - **ICLR**: International Conference on Learning Representations
   - **CVPR / ICCV**: Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition
   - **ACL / EMNLP**: Association for Computational Linguistics
