# Authoritative Academic & Industry Courses

World-class, openly accessible university lectures and structured engineering curricula.

## Stanford University
1. **Stanford CS229: Machine Learning**
   - *Instructor*: Andrew Ng / Tengyu Ma
   - *Scope*: Supervised learning, learning theory, generalization bounds, unsupervised learning, EM, and reinforcement learning.
   - *Platform*: Stanford Online & YouTube.

2. **Stanford CS224N: Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning**
   - *Instructor*: Christopher Manning
   - *Scope*: Word vectors, attention mechanisms, Transformers, pretraining, question answering, and LLM safety.
   - *Platform*: [web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/).

3. **Stanford CS231N: Deep Learning for Computer Vision**
   - *Scope*: Convolutions, spatial architectures, object detection, image segmentation, generative models, and visual attention.
   - *Platform*: [cs231n.stanford.edu/](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/).

4. **Stanford CS25: Transformers United**
   - *Scope*: Advanced multi-disciplinary seminars on Transformer scaling, FlashAttention, diffusion transformers, and multi-modal alignment.
   - *Platform*: YouTube / Stanford Online.

## UC Berkeley & MIT
5. **UC Berkeley CS188: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence**
   - *Scope*: Search algorithms, constraint satisfaction, adversarial games, MDPs, and reinforcement learning.
   - *Platform*: [inst.eecs.berkeley.edu/~cs188/](https://inst.eecs.berkeley.edu/~cs188/).

6. **MIT 6.S191: Introduction to Deep Learning**
   - *Scope*: Foundational neural networks, computer vision, sequence models, generative AI, and AI bias.
   - *Platform*: [introtodeeplearning.com](http://introtodeeplearning.com/).

## Applied & Industry Curricula
7. **Practical Deep Learning for Coders (fast.ai)**
   - *Instructor*: Jeremy Howard
   - *Scope*: Top-down pragmatic deep learning, computer vision, tabular modeling, collaborative filtering, and deployment.
   - *Platform*: [course.fast.ai](https://course.fast.ai/).

8. **Full Stack LLM Bootcamp**
   - *Scope*: Prompt engineering, RAG architectures, evaluation harnesses, vector databases, and production serving.
   - *Platform*: [fullstackdeeplearning.com](https://fullstackdeeplearning.com/).
