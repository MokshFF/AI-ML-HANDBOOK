# Authoritative AI Research Websites & Portals

Primary channels for discovering preprint papers, state-of-the-art benchmarks, and technical lab insights.

| Platform | Scope | Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **arXiv (cs.LG / cs.CL / cs.CV)** | Primary global repository for open access electronic preprints | [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/) |
| **Papers With Code** | Tracks state-of-the-art benchmarks, leaderboards, and code implementations | [paperswithcode.com](https://paperswithcode.com/) |
| **Hugging Face Daily Papers** | Community-curated feed of the most impactful daily AI research releases | [huggingface.co/papers](https://huggingface.co/papers) |
| **Distill.pub** | Peer-reviewed interactive visual explanations of machine learning research | [distill.pub](https://distill.pub/) |
| **Google DeepMind Research** | Landmark publications on AlphaFold, Gemini, reinforcement learning, and safety | [deepmind.google/research](https://deepmind.google/research/) |
| **OpenAI Research** | Technical reports and publications on GPT, DALL-E, Sora, and alignment | [openai.com/research](https://openai.com/research/) |
| **Anthropic Research** | Frontier research on Constitutional AI, mechanistic interpretability, and safety | [anthropic.com/research](https://www.anthropic.com/research) |
| **Meta AI (FAIR)** | Open-source research releases (LLaMA, PyTorch, Segment Anything) | [ai.meta.com/research](https://ai.meta.com/research/) |
