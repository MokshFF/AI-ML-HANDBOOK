# Audio Classification & Speaker Recognition - References & Further Reading

Seminal research papers, benchmark datasets, and authoritative literature on audio pattern recognition and speaker verification.

---

## 1. Seminal Research Papers

- **X-vectors: Robust DNN Embeddings for Speaker Recognition** (2018)
  - *Authors*: David Snyder, Daniel Garcia-Romero, Gregory Sell, Daniel Povey, Sanjeev Khudanpur
  - *Conference*: IEEE ICASSP 2018
  - *Contribution*: Established Time-Delay Neural Networks (TDNN) with temporal statistics pooling as the standard x-vector speaker embedding architecture.

- **PANNs: Large-Scale Pretrained Audio Neural Networks for Audio Pattern Recognition** (2020)
  - *Authors*: Qiuqiang Kong, Yin Cao, Haohe Liu, Keunwoo Choi, Yuxuan Wang
  - *Paper*: [arXiv:1912.10211](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.10211)
  - *Contribution*: Released pre-trained 2D CNN backbones on AudioSet achieving state-of-the-art across 6 audio classification benchmarks.

- **Generalized End-to-End Loss for Speaker Verification (GE2E)** (2018)
  - *Authors*: Li Wan, Quan Wang, Alan Papir, Ignacio Lopez Moreno (Google)
  - *Paper*: [arXiv:1710.10467](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10467)
  - *Contribution*: Introduced a scalable softmax-based metric learning loss that evaluates speaker centroids in a single pass, outperforming triplet loss.

- **VoxCeleb: A Large-Scale Speaker Identification Dataset** (2017)
  - *Authors*: Arsha Nagrani, Joon Son Chung, Andrew Zisserman
  - *Paper*: [arXiv:1706.08612](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.08612)
  - *Contribution*: Introduced the premier real-world in-the-wild video-derived benchmark dataset for speaker recognition.
