# Audio Fundamentals & DSP - References & Further Reading

Seminal textbooks, foundational papers, and authoritative documentation on digital signal processing, auditory perception, and speech acoustics.

---

## 1. Seminal Research Papers & Monograph Citations

- **A Scale for the Measurement of the Psychological Magnitude Pitch (The Mel Scale)** (1937)
  - *Authors*: S. S. Stevens, J. Volkmann, E. B. Newman
  - *Journal*: The Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, 8(3), 185-190.
  - *Contribution*: Established the perceptual Mel scale linking physical acoustic frequency to perceived pitch.

- **Comparison of Parametric Representations for Monosyllabic Word Recognition in Continuously Spoken Sentences (MFCC)** (1980)
  - *Authors*: Steven B. Davis, Paul Mermelstein
  - *Journal*: IEEE Transactions on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing, 28(4), 357-366.
  - *Contribution*: Standardized the Mel-Frequency Cepstrum Coefficients (MFCC) feature extraction pipeline for automatic speech recognition.

- **On the Use of Windows for Harmonic Analysis with the Discrete Fourier Transform** (1978)
  - *Author*: Fredric J. Harris
  - *Journal*: Proceedings of the IEEE, 66(1), 51-83.
  - *Contribution*: The seminal definitive survey on window functions (Hann, Hamming, Blackman-Harris) and spectral leakage trade-offs.

## 2. Textbooks & Authoritative Monographs

- **Discrete-Time Signal Processing (3rd ed.)**
  - *Authors*: Alan V. Oppenheim, Ronald W. Schafer (Pearson)
  - *Chapters*: Chapter 10 (Fourier Analysis of Signals using the DFT), Chapter 11 (Parametric Signal Modeling).
- **Speech and Audio Signal Processing: Processing and Perception of Speech and Music (2nd ed.)**
  - *Authors*: Ben Gold, Nelson Morgan, Dan Ellis (Wiley)
  - *Chapters*: Chapter 7 (Acoustic Phonetics), Chapter 10 (Short-Time Spectral Analysis).
