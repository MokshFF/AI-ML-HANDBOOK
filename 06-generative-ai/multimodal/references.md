# Multimodal Generative AI References & Seminal Papers

### Seminal Papers
- **Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision (CLIP)** (Radford et al., 2021)  
  *Introduced contrastive vision-language pretraining and zero-shot transfer.*  
  [https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020)

- **Visual Instruction Tuning (LLaVA)** (Liu et al., 2023)  
  *Pioneered lightweight projection adapters connecting vision backbones to open LLMs.*  
  [https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08485](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08485)

- **Classifier-Free Diffusion Guidance** (Ho & Salimans, 2022)  
  *Foundational formulation of guidance scaling without external classifiers.*  
  [https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.12598](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.12598)

- **High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models (Stable Diffusion)** (Rombach et al., 2022)  
  *Introduced latent diffusion models and cross-attention conditioning.*  
  [https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10752)

- **Flamingo: A Visual Language Model for Few-Shot Learning** (Alayrac et al., 2022)  
  *Cross-attention gating and Perceiver Resampler for interleaved vision-text sequences.*  
  [https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14198](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14198)

- **Robust Speech Recognition via Large-Scale Weak Supervision (Whisper)** (Radford et al., 2022)  
  [https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.04356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.04356)
